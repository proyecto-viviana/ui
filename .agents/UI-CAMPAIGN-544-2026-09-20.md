## Items 5–7, read against upstream before briefing

Done so the writer starts from the upstream answer instead of hunting. Item 5
is the one that matters: the ticket's wording is wrong and the tree is right.

**Item 7, `createId`.** Both twins — `solid-stately/src/ssr/index.ts:49` and
`solidaria/src/ssr/index.tsx:90` — return `defaultId` before generating.
Upstream `useId` always calls `useSSRSafeId` and only then chooses. In Solid 2
this is not merely a hook-order formality: `createUniqueId` is
`sharedConfig.hydrating ? sharedConfig.getNextContextId() : \`cl-${counter++}\``
(`solid-js/dist/solid.js:1159`), and **both** branches are order-dependent, so
skipping the call when a `defaultId` is present shifts every later generated id
in the same render or hydration pass. The fix is to hoist the call — and, in
solidaria's twin, the `SSRContext` read — above the branch.

**Item 6, `openLink`.** Ours (`solidaria/src/utils/dom.ts:572`) navigates:
`window.location.href = href`, or `window.open`. Upstream (`openLink.tsx:106-144`)
never navigates — it dispatches a `MouseEvent('click')` carrying the four
modifier keys on the anchor itself, after `focusWithoutScrolling(target)`, with
the `isOpening` latch around the dispatch. The signature diverges too: upstream
takes `(target, modifiers, setOpening = true)`, ours `(target, event, allowOpener)`.
`RouterProvider.tsx:112-123` already holds the faithful dispatch, so there will
be two copies; "never the third copy" says collapse them or record which is
canonical.

**Item 5, `ButtonGroup`.** The ticket says "regains its `children` dependency,
in both twins". Taken literally that reintroduces a bug the tree already
documents: both twins carry a comment at line 164 saying `local.children` must
not be read there, because `mergeProps` children getters instantiate JSX and
that is `PRIMITIVE_IN_FORBIDDEN_SCOPE` inside a tracked effect.

Upstream's intent still stands: S2 `ButtonGroup.tsx:157` puts `children` in
`checkForOverflow`'s dep array, and observes only the **parent** at line 175.
The real hole in ours is narrower than "no children dep" — when the group is
width-constrained, adding, removing or relabelling a child changes whether the
children overflow **without** changing the group's own border box, so neither
ResizeObserver fires. The Solid-native way to say upstream's dep is to watch
the rendered DOM rather than the getter: a `MutationObserver` on the group with
`childList`, `subtree` and `characterData`. That is not new tech here —
`solid-spectrum/src/breadcrumbs/index.tsx:252,515` already drives overflow
measurement exactly that way. Briefed to follow that shape, keep observing both
the group and the parent (a superset of upstream, and harmless), fix both twins
in one commit, and say in the log why the literal wording was not followed.
