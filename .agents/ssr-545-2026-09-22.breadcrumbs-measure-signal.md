## Fix

(viviana-ui `:267`, solid-spectrum `:266`). The `onSettled` block below it
already ran `setCanMeasure(canMeasureOverflow())` after commit, so the defect
was the initial value alone — no new lifecycle hook, no new export. The two
files stay a baselined diverged pair.

`HiddenBreadcrumbs` unconditionally at `:542` and measures in the layout effect
at `:527`. Its first render reads no environment either.
