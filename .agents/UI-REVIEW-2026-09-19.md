## Release-ready and excellence criteria

**Release-evidence ready** means one clean candidate has: terminating green unit, SSR,
hydrate, required interaction/a11y gates; #194-accepted 2,177/live inventory with
zero #537 waivers; fresh packed-artifact consumer proof; accepted measured
performance baseline and no ratchet regressions; #138/#139 closed; release
closure/version/integrity verified; exact-SHA evidence green. CSP and admin
hardening are release blockers only if the owner sets that policy or a reachable
exploit is demonstrated.

**Solid 2 initiative complete** additionally means #533 eliminates `access()`
from hot loops and standardizes reactive accessors, #535 applies SelectBox/Tree/
GridList slot classes at initial render without effect-time `querySelectorAll`
or child snapshots, and #536 removes the applicable defensive one-read/context
counter workarounds while all SSR/hydrate suites remain green. #531 orchestrates
#532–#537; child acceptance is not waived by earlier green runtime evidence.

**Parity excellence** additionally means #24 reaches 78/78 through current
runnable evidence, #25/#33 reach approved API disposition, D3/D13 remain zero
tolerance, and #34 keeps upstream changes absorbed on the same revision.

**Accessibility excellence** requires automated semantic/keyboard/focus/forms/
i18n/axe evidence plus recorded manual tasks for representative families across
applicable tested combinations of NVDA+Firefox, JAWS+Chrome, VoiceOver+Safari on
macOS/iOS, and TalkBack+Chrome on Android. Include keyboard-only journeys,
zoom/reflow and focus-not-obscured,
forced colors, reduced motion, touch targets/dismissal, names/descriptions/state,
validation recovery, live announcements, virtualized navigation, DnD, and
overlay focus return. Axe, AX-tree output, contrast-pair diffs, or screenshots
alone cannot establish WCAG/AT conformance.

**Performance excellence** means representative packed leaf imports have
accepted minified+brotli and forbidden-module budgets derived from repeated
baselines, collection/virtualizer scenarios have measured node/scroll/update
budgets against pinned upstream, and growth ratchets run in blocking CI.
The current five failures come from `scripts/check-entry-import-budget.ts` and
`scripts/entry-import-budget.json`; those source-controlled ceilings remain the
baseline until a reviewed, measurement-backed change.

**Security excellence** means a fresh strict advisory scan of the exact lockfile
and candidate revision, a recorded threat model for published packages, public
sites, local tools, and privileged release automation, no unresolved exploitable
high/critical finding in that scope, bounded destructive local tooling, and a
reproducible pinned release toolchain. This review did not run a fresh advisory
query and therefore makes no claim about current dependency vulnerability status.
It also includes #263's production-tested CSP as public defense in depth; its
release-blocking status remains owner policy absent a reachable injection sink.
