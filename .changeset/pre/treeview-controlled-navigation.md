Add controlled TreeView focus through `focusedKey` and `onFocusChange`, independently of selection. Support custom keyboard navigation and letter search through `keyboardDelegate`, and allow type-ahead to be disabled with `disallowTypeAhead`. Existing controlled expansion uses `expandedKeys` and `onExpandedChange`; row controls remain reachable with `keyboardNavigationBehavior="tab"`.
